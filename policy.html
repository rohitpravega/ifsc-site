Complaint Handling and Grievance Redressal Policy

Last updated on 20th November 2026

1) Objective

This policy aims to establish a robust, transparent and efficient mechanism for receiving, handling and redressing complaints and grievances of Consumers. It ensures compliance with regulatory requirements, enhances consumer trust and promotes accountability within the organisation.

2) Scope

This policy applies to all complaints and grievances arising from the services offered by 9Main Managers LLP (IFSC Branch) (“FME”).

3) Definitions

For the purposes of this policy

  1. Complaint / Grievance shall mean and include:
    1. A written expression of dissatisfaction against the operation of the Fund/Scheme or its employees resulting from a potential damage or wrongdoing to a Customer or from a violation of IFSCA guidelines or related laws/regulations by FME which may or may not request for a claim for compensation.

      Explanation: An interaction or communication shall be formally classified and registered as a Complaint only after the concerned FME personnel has identified and recorded, in writing, the source and type of error or issue giving rise to such interaction.

      It does not include: -

      1. Anonymous complaints (except whistleblower complaints)
      2. Incomplete or un-specific complaints
      3. Allegations without supporting documents
      4. Suggestions or seeking guidance/explanation
      5. Complaints on matters not relating to the financial products or services provided by the FME
      6. Complaints about any unregistered/ un-regulated activity
      7. References in the nature of seeking information or clarifications about financial products or service.
  2. Governing Body shall mean the Board of Partners of the LLP
  3. Consumer shall have the same meaning as assigned to “Client” or “Customer” under clause 1.3.11 of the IFSCA (Anti Money Laundering, Counter-Terrorist Financing and Know Your Customer) Guidelines, 2022;

4) Complaint Registration

  1. Registration of Complaints
    1. Modes of registering grievances: Customer can file a complaint via any one of the modes.
      1. In writing through E- mail to the id devesh@pravegavc.com
      2. In writing via letters to the following address:

        Unit No.126, Seat Nos. 1 to 4, Ground Floor,Pragya Accelerator,15B, Block-15,Road no.1C,,Zone-1, GFT SEZ , GIFT City,Gandhinagar,Gujarat,India,382050

    2. Complaint raising by Customer: While raising a complaint each complaint should have the following information:
      1. Customer’s complete name
      2. Customer’s complete correspondence address
      3. Contact number
      4. e-mail address
      5. Detail of the complaint

5) Resolution of complaints

  1. Authority: The Governing body shall appoint a Complaint Redressal Officer (CRO) and Complaint Redressal Appellate Officer (CRAO).
  2. Receipt and Acknowledgment:
    1. The FME shall provide an acknowledgement to the customer within 3 working days, if accepted.
    2. If not accepted, the same shall be communicated within 5 working days, along with reasons for rejection.
  3. Investigation, Resolution and closure of complaints:
    1. Complaints shall be investigated in a fair, transparent, and impartial manner
    2. Complaints shall be resolved within 15 working days, but not later than 30 working days, from the date of acceptance
    3. If the customer does not come back within 7 working days for reopening of the case, the complaint will be considered as closed.
  4. Additional Information: The FME may request additional information from complainants to process complaints effectively.

6) Appeal Mechanism

  1. Level 1:
    1. Dissatisfied complainants will have the option to escalate to the next level by filing an appeal with the CRAO within 21 days of receiving the CRO’s decision.
    2. The CRAO shall dispose of appeals within 30 days.
  2. Level 2:
    1. If the consumer remains dissatisfied after the response of the Level 1 Appeal, the complaint can be escalated with the IFSCA. Such escalation can be made by filing a complaint before the Authority through email to grievance-redressal@ifsca.gov.in preferably within 21 days from receipt of the decision from the FME. Further details will be displayed on the website of the company.

7) Record Keeping

  1. The FME shall maintain all records relating to handling of complaints, including the following
    1. Complaints received and processed;
    2. All correspondence exchanged between the FME and the complainants;
    3. all information and documents examined and relied upon by the FME while processing of the complaints;
    4. Outcome of the complaints;
    5. Reasons for rejection of complaints, if any
    6. Timelines for processing of complaints; and
    7. Data of all complaints handled by it
  2. Record shall be maintained for at least six years from the date of disposal of complaint. In case of any pending litigation or legal proceeding relating to the complaint, the records shall be maintained for the applicable period, after final disposal of the proceeding
  3. Records of complaints, including correspondence, evidence, resolutions, and timelines, shall be maintained for at least six years or longer in case of ongoing litigation.
  4. Records shall be maintained in an electronic retrieval system.

8) Reporting

  1. The FME shall submit reports on complaints and resolutions to IFSCA as required.
  2. The Annual Report, if any, to be submitted to IFSCA shall include a section titled “Complaint Handling and Grievance Redressal”, presenting data on complaints received, resolved, rejected, and pending.

9) Review and Amendments

This Policy shall be reviewed periodically in accordance with applicable laws and at such intervals as the FME may deem necessary. Any modification to this Policy shall be appropriately documented and approved by the Board.
